SAND Program

Student Ambassador Networking & Development (SAND) connects UC San Diego students with paid and perk-based brand ambassador roles.

Open Ambassador Programs

Company Role Pay Deadline
Tide Pool Energy Brand Ambassador Paid + product Nov 30
Coastline Tech Campus Tech Ambassador $18/hr Dec 1
Seashell Skincare Beauty Ambassador Free product Dec 15

How to Apply

  1. Pick brands you already use and love.
  2. Update your resume and LinkedIn.
  3. Show the orgs and communities you can reach.
  4. Bring one creative campus marketing idea to the interview.

Events to Table At

Ambassadors can market their brands at these campus events.

  • Winter Involvement Fair: Thu, Jan 7, Library Walk
  • Winter Career Fair: Wed, Jan 27, Price Center Plaza

For Companies

Questions about having student ambassadors at UC San Diego? Email sand@example.edu.
